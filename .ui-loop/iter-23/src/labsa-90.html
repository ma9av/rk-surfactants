Introduction to LABSA 90%

Industrial LABSA 90% or Linear Alkyl Benzene Sulfonic Acid, is a crucial chemical compound that plays a significant role in various industrial applications.

In this informative article, we delve deep into the world of Industrial LABSA 90%, providing a detailed overview of its properties, applications, production process, and more.

Let’s explore the many facets of this versatile chemical.

What we dispatch

We sulphonate linear alkyl benzene with 98% sulphuric acid and dispatch a light brown liquid, readily soluble in water. Detergent-cake, powder and liquid-cleaner plants buy it as acid slurry and neutralize it on their line. 90 ± 1% active matter is why a lot from this plant does not force reformulation.

  • 75 MT a day (~2,275 MT a month)
  • 300 MT raw / 200 MT finished storage
  • Own RKSPL VALSAD tanker
  • Waste acid sold to fertilizer plants
  • Partners move around 50 MT a day
  • ISO 9001:2015

We sell the acid, not the salt

We dispatch the sulphonic acid. Plants make the salt (LAS) on their own line. We do not sell the neutralized paste.

Applications

Detergent plants

About 700–800 MT goes to cake and powder plants in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Kerala, for cakes, powder, liquids and dishwashing soaps.

Textile

Specified as a wetting agent so dyes spread evenly on the fibre. Same CoA lots as cake plants, not a separate grade.

Agriculture

Formulators add the acid so herbicides and pesticides wet the leaf. Metric tonnes only; we do not pack 50 kg agro bags.

Leather

Tanneries use it to carry dye into the hide. Same free-acid 5.5–6.5 and Klett 20–25 as detergent lots.

How it is made

Four steps on the vessels. Plant, tanks and equipment

  1. 01
    Charge LAB

    Linear alkyl benzene is held in its own labelled tank. Raw storage on site is 300 MT.

  2. 02
    Sulphonate

    LAB is reacted with 98% sulphuric acid in the sulphonation vessels. Spent acid from this step is sold to fertilizer plants.

  3. 03
    Separate

    The mixture goes through the separation vessels and aquators on site, then to finished-goods tanks.

  4. 04
    Test and fill

    The lab checks active matter, free acid and Klett colour. Finished LABSA 90% is stored (200 MT finished-goods capacity).
