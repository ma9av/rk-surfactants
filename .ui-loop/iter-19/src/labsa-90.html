How it is made

Four steps on the vessels at Plot 79/80. Plant, tanks and equipment

  1. 01
    Charge LAB

    Held in a labelled tank. Raw storage 300 MT.

  2. 02
    Sulphonate

    Reacted with 98% sulphuric acid.

  3. 03
    Separate

    Separation vessels and aquators on site.

  4. 04
    Test and fill

    Lab checks active matter, free acid and Klett. Then 200 MT finished storage, barrel-filled.
