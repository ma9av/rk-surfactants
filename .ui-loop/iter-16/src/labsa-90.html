LABSA 90% from Gundlav

Linear alkyl benzene sulphonic acid, 90 ± 1% active matter, made at our plant in Gundlav, Valsad.

What LABSA 90% is

LABSA 90% is linear alkyl benzene sulphonic acid — the anionic surfactant detergent-cake, powder and liquid-cleaner plants buy as acid slurry. At Plot 79/80 we sulphonate linear alkyl benzene with 98% sulphuric acid and dispatch a light brown liquid that is readily soluble in water.

Buyers neutralize that acid to make the washing-active salt in their formula. The same 90 ± 1% active matter is why a lot from this plant does not force reformulation.

  • 90 ± 1% active matter Checked on every batch in the Gundlav laboratory before dispatch.
  • Klett 20–25 as dispatched Colour and free acid are read in the same lab as the active-matter titration.
  • Sold in metric tonnes Barrel-filled at the plant. Ask for drum size with the quantity.

Specification

Values as dispatched from Plot 79/80, taken from the company-profile analytical report: active matter 90 ± 1, free sulphuric acid 5.5–6.5, colour 20–25 Klett.

We sell the acid, not the salt

At Plot 79/80 we sulphonate linear alkyl benzene with 98% sulphuric acid. Detergent plants neutralize that acid on their own line to make the salt used in cakes, powders and liquids. That step is theirs; the 90 ± 1% active matter they start from is ours.

Where plants put LABSA 90%

Most of our tonnage goes into detergent cakes and powders. The same acid is also specified as a wetting agent and emulsifier in textile, agrochemical and leather formulations. We do not pack consumer sizes.

Detergent cakes, powders and liquids

About 700–800 MT from Plot 79/80 currently goes to detergent-cake and powder plants in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Kerala. Buyers neutralize the acid on their line for cakes, powder, liquid detergents and dishwashing soaps.

Textile processing

A minority of the same CoA lots are specified as a wetting agent for dyes. Same 90 ± 1% active matter; not a separate grade.

Agrochemical sprays

Same acid, same metric-tonne order, used so sprays wet the leaf. We do not pack an agro grade.

Leather processing

Same light-brown liquid from Plot 79/80, used to carry dye into the hide. Same free-acid and Klett limits as detergent lots.

How it is made at Gundlav

Four steps on the vessels at Plot 79/80. Waste acid from the process is sold to fertilizer plants. Present capacity is 75 MT a day, about 2,275 MT a month on three shifts.

Plant, tanks and equipment

  1. Charge LAB Linear alkyl benzene is held in its own labelled tank. Raw storage on site is 300 MT.
  2. Sulphonate LAB is reacted with 98% sulphuric acid in the sulphonation vessels.
  3. Separate The mixture goes through separation vessels and aquators.
  4. Test and fill The lab checks active matter, free acid and Klett colour. Finished LABSA 90% is stored (200 MT finished-goods capacity) and barrel-filled for dispatch.

Questions buyers ask

Short answers from the Gundlav desk. Call +91 97277 02597 if the lot size or the destination is the real question.

What does LABSA stand for?

Linear alkyl benzene sulphonic acid. At this plant it is sold as LABSA 90%, 90 ± 1% active matter, light brown, readily soluble in water.

Do you sell it for cosmetics or household use?

No. We manufacture and sell in metric tonnes for industrial detergent and cleaning formulations. We do not pack consumer or cosmetic sizes.

Which plants buy LABSA 90% from Gundlav?

Detergent-cake and powder plants are the core. Present supply is about 700–800 MT to Gujarat, Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Kerala. The same acid is also specified in textile, agrochemical and leather blends.

Is linear alkyl benzene the biodegradable feedstock?

Yes. We sulphonate linear alkyl benzene, not branched alkyl benzene. Downstream handling still follows the SDS we send with a document request.

How is it stored and moved?

Finished LABSA sits in dedicated tanks on site. Dispatch is barrel-filled at the plant, or on our RKSPL VALSAD tanker. Transport partners can move around 50 MT a day.
