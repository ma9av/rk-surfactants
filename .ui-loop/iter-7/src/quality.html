Quality

Every batch of LABSA 90% is tested in our in-house laboratory at Gundlav before dispatch.

In-house laboratory

Testing equipment includes a photo-electric colorimeter and a titration system. The laboratory also has an analytical balance and a hot air oven. The same lab checks sulphuric acid as well as LABSA.

We test against the published LABSA 90% specification: active matter, non-digestible oil matter, free sulphuric acid, moisture and colour (Klett).

What each batch is checked for

The same limits as on the specification page, taken from the company profile analytical report. A sample certificate of analysis is available on request.

Active matter90 ± 1% by weight
Non-digestible oil matter1% max
Free sulphuric acid5.5–6.5% by weight
Moisture3.0–4.0% by weight
Colour20–25 Klett, when dispatched
