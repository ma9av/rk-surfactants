Leadership

Mr. Binay Chandra Jha

Chairman

Mr. Binay Chandra Jha is an entrepreneur with 25 years of experience in the detergent industry and in LABSA plants. He holds a graduate degree from Lalit Narayan Mithila University (LNMU), Bihar, along with professional certifications from various institutions.

He is a firm believer in lifelong learning. Even in his fifties, driven by curiosity and a commitment to self-development, he enrolled in environmental studies through an open schooling programme.

Mrs. Sulekha Binaychandra Jha

Director

Mrs. Sulekha Binaychandra Jha brings over a decade of leadership experience across business organisations, with expertise in marketing, sales and procurement. She is known for a people-centric approach and a strong belief in teamwork.

Her work on sales, procurement and operations has helped the company supply LABSA 90% from the Gundlav plant to detergent makers in India.

Dr. Ravi Jha

Executive Director

Dr. Ravi Jha is a medical professional pursuing his postgraduate specialisation in Orthopaedic Surgery, while contributing to the strategic development and growth of the family-owned business.

He combines a medical background with an entrepreneurial mindset. His interests include strategic planning, business diversification and exploring opportunities across emerging industries.

Mission and vision

Mission

To supply LABSA 90% (90 ± 1% active matter) from our own Gundlav plant, tested batch by batch in our laboratory, at a price detergent makers can run with.

Vision

To keep the Gundlav plant a reliable LABSA source for Indian detergent and cleaning-product makers, through in-house testing, ISO 9001:2015 practice, and long working relationships with customers and suppliers.

How we work

  • Own plant, not a trading desk. LABSA 90% is made at Plot 79/80, Gundlav GIDC, Valsad. Present capacity 75 MT a day.
  • Batch testing. Active matter, oil matter, free sulphuric acid, moisture and Klett colour are checked in our lab before dispatch.
  • Named people. Chairman Binay Chandra Jha (25 years in detergents and LABSA plants), Director Sulekha Binaychandra Jha, Executive Director Dr. Ravi Jha.
  • Documents on request. Technical data sheet, safety data sheet, sample CoA, ISO 9001:2015 certificate, and the company profile (PDF).
