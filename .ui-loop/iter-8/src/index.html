The plant

The factory is at Plot No. 79/80, Gundlav Notified Industrial Estate, GIDC Gundlav, Valsad. LABSA is made from linear alkyl benzene and 98% sulphuric acid. Present capacity is 75 MT a day, about 2,275 MT a month on three shifts, with expansion toward 150 MT a day. We run our own tanker marked RKSPL VALSAD, and work with transport companies that can move around 50 MT a day.

Every batch is tested

Batches are checked in our own laboratory with a photo-electric colorimeter and a titration system, plus an analytical balance and a hot air oven. Radha Krishna Surfactants Pvt. Ltd. is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company.
