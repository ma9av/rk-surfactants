Tested before it leaves

No batch is dispatched until our own lab has checked it. A photo-electric colorimeter reads the Klett colour, and titration confirms active matter and free acid. An analytical balance and hot air oven handle the rest. The plant runs under ISO 9001:2015, so the same checks happen the same way every time.
