Manufacturing at Gundlav, Valsad

The plant sits in Gundlav Notified Industrial Estate, GIDC Gundlav. Present capacity is 75 MT of LABSA a day, about 2,275 MT a month on three shifts.

Raw materials and product tanks

Each material has its own tank, labelled on site: LAB (linear alkyl benzene), sulphuric acid, and finished LABSA. Raw material storage is 300 MT. Finished goods storage is 200 MT of LABSA and diluted sulphuric acid. Waste acid from the process is sold to fertilizer plants.

How LABSA is made here

The process uses the vessels on site. Packing is by barrel filling at the plant. Ask us for the packing size you need with your quantity.

  1. Charge LAB Linear alkyl benzene is held in its own labelled tank (300 MT raw storage in total).
  2. Sulphonate LAB is reacted with 98% sulphuric acid in the sulphonation vessels.
  3. Separate The mixture goes through separation vessels and aquators.
  4. Fill Finished LABSA 90% is stored (200 MT) and filled into barrels for dispatch.

Dispatch from Plot 79/80

We run our own tanker marked RKSPL VALSAD, and we have a tie-up with transport companies that can move around 50 MT of LABSA a day. About 700–800 MT currently goes to plants in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Kerala.

The plant is expanding toward 150 MT a day. Consent from the Gujarat Pollution Control Board and environmental clearance from the Ministry of Environment are in place.
