Leadership

Chairman – Radha Krishna Surfactants Private Limited

Mr. Binay Chandra Jha is a visionary entrepreneur with extensive experience in the detergent industry and its associated raw materials. He holds a graduate degree from Lalit Narayan Mithila University (LNMU), along with additional professional certifications from reputed institutions.

A firm believer in lifelong learning, he has shown that the pursuit of knowledge has no age limit. Even in his fifties, driven by curiosity and continuous self-development, he enrolled in environmental studies through an open schooling programme.

Knowledge can be acquired anytime, anywhere, and at any age. Learning never goes to waste; knowledge always rewards you in one way or another. Keep Learning, Keep Growing.

He founded Radha Krishna Surfactants Private Limited on two principles: uncompromising commitment to quality, and continuous learning and improvement. Under his leadership the company has strengthened its manufacturing capabilities, built lasting business relationships, and expanded its presence in Indian LABSA manufacturing.

Director – Radha Krishna Surfactants Private Limited

Mrs. Sulekha Binaychandra Jha brings over a decade of leadership experience across business organisations, with expertise in marketing, sales and procurement.

Known for her confident leadership, people-centric approach and strong belief in teamwork, she inspires people to perform with dedication and keep high standards of quality.

Her strategic contribution and commitment to operational excellence have strengthened the company's performance, fostered business growth and expanded its presence in the Indian LABSA manufacturing industry. Present supply is about 700–800 MT to detergent plants all over India.

Executive Director – Radha Krishna Surfactants Private Limited

Dr. Ravi Jha is a medical professional currently pursuing his postgraduate specialization in Orthopaedic Surgery, while actively contributing to the strategic development and growth of the family-owned business, Radha Krishna Surfactants Private Limited.

Combining his medical background with an entrepreneurial mindset, he brings a multidisciplinary perspective to leadership and business development. Beyond medicine, his interests extend to entrepreneurship, strategic planning, business diversification, and exploring opportunities across emerging industries.

He believes in continuous learning, innovation, and creating sustainable ventures, with a vision to build a strong foundation for long-term professional and business growth.
